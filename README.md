BrowserBabyAlarm

A secure, peer-to-peer (P2P) web-based baby monitor running entirely in the browser with zero external media storage.

Overview

BrowserBabyAlarm is a modern, privacy-first web application designed to turn spare smartphones and computers into a reliable, encrypted baby monitor without requiring app store installations or paid subscriptions.

Because it leverages WebRTC (Peer-to-Peer) and the Web Audio API, your video and audio streams flow directly between devices on your local network. No video recordings are ever saved on cloud servers.

Key Features

100% P2P & Private: Direct encrypted browser-to-browser streaming. No third-party video storage.

Smart VOX (Crying Detection): Real-time RMS audio analysis with adjustable sensitivity thresholds and visual decibel meters.

Auto-Reconnect & Heartbeat Watcher: Robust background monitoring that detects sudden connection drops and triggers a loud visual/audio alarm.

OLED Blackout Mode: Completely turns the baby phone's screen pitch black on OLED displays to save battery and prevent light emission.

Built-in Sleep Sound Generator: Generates soothing ambient sounds directly from the baby unit (Womb / Brown noise, Gentle Rain, Ocean Waves, Heartbeat, and Lullaby).

Push-to-Talk Intercom: Hold down a button on the parent device to speak directly through the baby phone's speaker.

Multi-Language Support: Easily toggle between Danish (DA) and English (EN) directly from the header interface.
